**ClickMeter 1.0.2** for Windows 10/11 (x64) · Free for personal use

### What's new

- Clicks are now counted through a low-level mouse hook — every individual click registers, including rapid or injected bursts that browser-based testers miss entirely
- The hook runs on its own dedicated thread — measuring adds effectively zero overhead, so ClickMeter never slows down the input it's measuring
- Counts left / right / middle clicks from any source — physical, automation, or injected
- Window no longer steals keyboard focus — your hotkeys (and other apps') keep working while ClickMeter is open
- Fixed ClickMeter closing when captured with Snipping Tool or other screen-capture tools
